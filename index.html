AI 正在以前所未有的速度改变每一个行业的工作方式。AI is changing how every industry works at an unprecedented pace.

早学者持续受益Early adopters keep gaining

使用门槛极低A very low barrier

无需技术背景,无需编程能力,能够打字即可上手。No technical background or coding skills required—if you can type, you can use it.
